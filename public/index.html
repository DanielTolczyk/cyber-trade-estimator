Blueprints, Not Checkboxes.

Cybersecurity is an applied operational trade. Just as electricians, aviation mechanics, and structural engineers maintain critical physical infrastructure, cybersecurity practitioners build and defend the critical digital lifelines of modern society.

No more checkbox compliance or unpaid internships. Use this tool to calculate your verified runtime hours, Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) credits, and standing in a licensed, worker-first trade framework.

How the Trade Placement Engine Works

This tool estimates where your existing career experience, military background, degrees, and certifications place you under the National Cybersecurity Trade Board standards. Hover over any highlighted term (like PLA, RTI, AMF, or MoR) for instant definitions.
